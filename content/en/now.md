A “now” page is a place to share what has your attention lately. It can change as often—or as rarely—as you do.

## Making

Setting up this little notebook. Starting with a few honest pages and letting the rest take shape over time.

## Learning

How to keep notes that are useful to a future self. Less collecting, more connecting.

## Leaving room for

Unplanned walks, unfinished books, and projects that don’t need to become anything bigger.
