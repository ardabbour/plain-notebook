This page is yours to fill. Introduce yourself the way you would to someone sitting across the table.

You don’t need a polished personal brand. A name, a few interests, and something you’re curious about are a good beginning.

## A short introduction

Try three sentences: what occupies your days, what you care about, and what you’re learning. Leave room for who you are outside your work.

## Things I’m drawn to

- Work made with care.
- Learning by making something small.
- Conversations that leave you with a new question.
- Finding a good explanation and passing it along.

These are example interests. Swap them for the things that feel like you.

## Get in touch

Add a contact link you’re comfortable making public: an email address, a professional profile, or another place people can find you.

::: note A note on privacy
You get to choose how much of yourself goes here. A useful personal website doesn’t need your address, your schedule, or your whole life story.
:::
