## The idea

Make a place for useful passages, observations, and questions. Organize them by the ideas they connect to, so they become more than a pile of saved links.

## The approach

Start with a few pages. For each entry, include the source, the passage or observation, and a sentence about why it matters to you. Link related entries together.

## What to put here

When you write about your own project, show what you made, explain an interesting decision, and share what changed along the way. Add images or a live link if they help tell the story.
